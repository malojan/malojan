# Malo Jan

PhD candidate in political science at Sciences Po (Centre for European Studies and Comparative Politics). I study climate politics, party competition and political communication, using computational text analysis and survey data.

**Data and replication materials**

- [PartySOME](https://github.com/malojan/partysome): political parties' social media activity and issue agendas across Europe (with Luis Sattelmayer)
- [Executive Climate Change Attention](https://github.com/malojan/executive_climate_change_attention): climate change attention in UN General Assembly speeches, 1985–2024 (with Emiliano Grossman)
- [French manifestos](https://github.com/malojan/french_manifestos): French party manifestos at the sentence level, with policy-topic classification
- [Symbolic climate policies](https://github.com/malojan/symbolic_climate_policy): replication materials (with Théodore Tallent and Luis Sattelmayer)

**Teaching**

- [Introduction to R](https://github.com/malojan/introduction-r) for political science students
- [Introduction to web scraping](https://github.com/malojan/intro_webscraping)
